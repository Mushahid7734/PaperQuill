About PaperQuill

A private research writing studio for solo researchers. Your drafts stay in this browser (IndexedDB) with optional disk JSON backups. No accounts, no tracking servers for your prose.

  • Citations (MLA, APA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver) with live re-render
  • Shared source library across papers · DOI & OpenAlex · BibTeX/RIS import
  • Tutorial mode, templates, LanguageTool, quote bank, snapshots
  • Export DOCX, PDF (print), Markdown, HTML

Tip: open Tools for LanguageTool, Tutorial, Templates, and the rest of the studio toolkit.
