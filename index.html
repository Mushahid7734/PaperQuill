About PaperQuill

A private research writing studio for solo researchers. Drafts stay in this browser (IndexedDB) with optional disk backups and Google Drive sync. Sign in with Google is only needed for PaperQuill Pro; no account is required for local writing.

  • Citations (MLA, APA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver)
  • Sources, multi-library search (OpenAlex / Crossref / Europe PMC / Semantic Scholar / DOAJ), DOI lookup, tutorial mode, LanguageTool, snapshots
  • Export DOCX, PDF, Markdown, HTML · Drive sync
